Practicum No. 6: Positioning Elements

1. Static Positioning (Default)

Static elements follow the standard document flow. Offset properties (top, right, bottom, left) have no effect.

I am a static element.

2. Relative Positioning

Relative elements are offset from their normal position in the document flow without affecting surrounding elements.

I am positioned relatively (top: 15px, left: 20px).

3. Absolute Positioning

Absolute elements are removed from the document flow and positioned relative to their nearest positioned ancestor.

Parent Container (position: relative)

I am positioned absolutely (bottom: 15px, right: 15px).

4. Fixed & Sticky Positioning

Fixed: The red badge at the top right of the viewport remains locked in place during scroll.

Sticky: The blue section title bar sticks to the top of the window as you scroll through the page.
